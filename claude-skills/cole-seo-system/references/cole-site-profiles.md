# Site Profiles

## cole.edu.vn
- Type: education content hub
- Primary goals:
  - topical authority
  - course conversion
  - article refresh
  - internal linking
  - GEO/citability improvements
- Typical page types:
  - “X là gì” definition articles
  - roadmap articles
  - comparison pages
  - topic/category hubs
  - course landing pages
- Preferred SEO style:
  - answer intent early
  - clear H2 structure
  - readable examples/tables/lists
  - CTA after value delivery

## softwareengineer.vn
- Type: engineering content publisher / app-backed site
- Primary goals:
  - technical hygiene
  - stable rendering
  - consistent templates
  - article quality
  - schema correctness
- Typical page types:
  - technical explainer articles
  - category pages
  - homepage/card layouts
  - route/template-driven pages
- Preferred SEO style:
  - stable title/meta patterns
  - consistent H1/H2 hierarchy
  - reliable images and metadata
  - low crawl waste
